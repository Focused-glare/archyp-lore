# PUBLICATION 1. THREE-ZONE TOPOLOGY: WHERE THE STATE ENDS AND SOVEREIGNTY BEGINS

### From the Author: From Artistic Vision to Direct Action

This publication cycle is not merely a series of theoretical reflections, but a condensed, systematic, and simplified adaptation of materials from the second volume of my novel trilogy. The initial concept envisioned a detailed, leisurely exposition of these ideas through artistic imagery and the fates of characters, where each legal and philosophical nuance would mature naturally.

However, the aggressive and accelerated pace of real-world events broke this schedule. Radical upheavals in the legal framework, the implementation of total digital levers, and the dismantling of fundamental guarantees of freedom are happening right now. We do not have time to wait for the completion of a major literary form. The Manifesto of 21st Century Justice enters the world as a direct instrument of response to present-day challenges—without artistic metaphors, but with a clear system architecture.

### Bridge: Inversion of the Digital Aquarium

Recent government initiatives under the slogans of a "compact, digital, and efficient state," combined with laws on automated debt collection and asset freezing (such as Law No. 4833-IX), finally expose the primary vector of the current system. The state machine is building a one-sided digital aquarium where the citizen becomes fully transparent, stripped of the right to immediate protection of property and personal space. Meanwhile, the state itself, its apparatus, and systemic capital retain immunity and protection from coercion.

The response to this arbitrariness is a fundamental shift in social architecture—the Three-Zone Topology, which turns the digital searchlight 180 degrees: from monitoring the human being to total monitoring of the state itself.

---

### Section 1. Anatomy of Friction and the Three-Zone Topology

To neutralize the threat of total surveillance, social architecture is divided into three clearly demarcated zones with distinct sovereign statuses:

#### 1. Personal Zone (Absolute "Dark" Zone)
* **Scope:** Housing, private devices, biometric data, thoughts, health status, family and personal relationships.
* **Legal Status:** Complete digital and physical sovereignty of the citizen. Access of any external monitoring algorithms, automated registries, or executive bodies to this zone equals zero.
* **Technical Safeguard:** Sensors, smart devices, and internal computing nodes inside the home are deprived of external data transmission ports by default. No state system has the right to demand "opening a peephole" into private life.

#### 2. Private / Commercial Zone (Free Market Sphere)
* **Scope:** Contractual relations, entrepreneurship, free exchange of property, services, and capital.
* **Legal Status:** Zone of economic freedom and voluntary agreements.
* **Operational Mechanism:** Operating according to rules of transparent economic audit of transaction facts. Accounting for cash flow and tax payments occurs without collecting, aggregating, or storing personal data about the private lives of owners or transaction participants. Businesses are accountable for financial performance outcomes, not surrendering personal data to constant surveillance.

#### 3. Public Zone (Sphere of 100% Transparency)
* **Scope:** State positions, budget funds, municipal property, regulatory bodies, and natural resources.
* **Legal Status:** Absolute transparency without the right to privacy within the scope of executing public functions.
* **Operational Mechanism:** Any entity taking an oath or entering this zone (official, MP, judge, government contractor) comes under a continuous, sterile monitoring searchlight. All assets, transactions, decisions, and contacts of an official in the public sphere are real-time open data.

### 📌 MARGINAL SCHOLIA (To Section 1)
**Regarding the Infrastructural Implementation of Digital Coercion:**

Government declarations about "digitalization and transparency" create only a legal and conceptual framework. The technological execution of this surveillance is realized through separate interdepartmental agreements and memorandums (in particular, practices of utilizing Big Data analytical platforms like Palantir or their equivalents in the structures of the Ministry of Digital Transformation, Ministry of Defense, and Ministry of Economy).

The official text of government programs usually operates with general terms ("optimization of registries," "automated risk analysis"), yet through mechanisms of subordinate acts, private and military analytical engines are connected to these registries. This transforms the Unified Register of Debtors and banking databases from informational references into instruments of algorithmic property destruction targeting citizens.
